Backed by his 18 years experience, Dr. Kalpesh Purbiya specializes in stroke and slip disc rehabilitation. treated over 5,000 patients and earned state awards for free camps. Known as Udaipur’s "Pain Magician," offers advanced therapy and online consultations too. Known as the ‘Pain Magician’, Dr. Kalpesh Purbiya has been dedicated to the field of Physiotherapy for the past 18 years. After completing Bachelor's Degree, established clinic in 2007 and has since become known for his focused expertise in treating orthopedic and neurological conditions.

When I started the clinic back in 2007, Physiotherapy was still a relatively unfamiliar concept for many people. With very few patients in the beginning, Dr. Purbiya took it upon himself to raise awareness about the importance and effectiveness of physiotherapy. Through consistent hard work, organized numerous free physiotherapy camps, for which he was honored with a state-level award in 2015.
