Clinical Profile

Dr. Kalpesh Purbiya

Udaipur's First Chiropractor • Spine & Neuro Rehab Specialist

BPT MPT (NEURO) MIAP COMP MMTFI Osteopath Spine Expert

Popularly hailed by patients as Udaipur's "Pain Magician", Dr. Kalpesh Purbiya has dedicated his career to non-invasive musculoskeletal and spine medicine since establishing his clinic in 2007.

He completed his Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) and Master of Physiotherapy specializing in Neurology (MPT Neuro), followed by specialized fellowships in manual therapy, osteopathy, and spine manipulation. He has mastered the internationally acclaimed Australian Maitland & Mulligan Therapy approaches for cervical and lumbar radiculopathy.

State Award in 2015

Honored with a prestigious Rajasthan State-Level Award for conducting community-wide free medical physiotherapy camps.

5,000+ Recoveries

Treated more than 5,000 patients suffering from severe slip disc, sciatica, neck spasms, and chronic pain without surgery.
