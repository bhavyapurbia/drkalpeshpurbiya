About the Specialist

Dr. Kalpesh Purbiya

Udaipur's First Chiropractor • Spine & Neuro Rehab Expert

BPT MPT (NEURO) MIAP COMP MMTFI Osteopath Spine Expert

Known respectfully across South Rajasthan as the ‘Pain Magician’, Dr. Kalpesh Purbiya has been practicing advanced physiotherapy and spine therapy since 2007. With mastery in Australian Maitland & Mulligan joint mobilization techniques, he specializes in treating complex cervical & lumbar radiculopathy without surgery.

  • Pioneering Care: Established in 2007 when modern physiotherapy was still emerging; organized numerous free treatment camps and honored with a State-Level Award in 2015.
  • Comprehensive Spine Solutions: Non-invasive correction for slip disc, sciatica, nerve impingement, and chronic back/neck stiffness.
  • Neurological & Stroke Recovery: High-precision rehabilitation protocols designed to regain motor strength and balance.
